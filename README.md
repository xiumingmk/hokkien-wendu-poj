# Pan-Min-Nan POJ diasystem auto-deriver
## 泛閩南語白話字綜合音系自動推導器

**An idealized literary (文讀) Hokkien reading for every Chinese character, written in Pe̍h-ōe-jī (POJ), derived from Middle Chinese.**
〔以中古漢語（《廣韻》）推導的理想化閩南語文讀，用白話字書寫。〕

## In plain words

Hokkien has two ways of reading most characters. The everyday (白讀) reading is the spoken word: 大 is *tōa* in "big". The literary (文讀) reading is the one used for reading classical texts and poetry aloud: 大 is *tāi* in 大學. Dictionaries record the literary readings only for some characters, and Amoy, Taiwan, Zhangzhou, Quanzhou and Penang each keep slightly different things apart.

This script gives you one literary reading for any character you give it, so you can read a classical text aloud in Hokkien without looking up each character. It starts from the Middle Chinese sound of the character (from the 廣韻, via the NK2028 tshet-uinh framework) and applies rules that say how those sounds come out in literary Hokkien.

### Why it is built the way it is

No single Hokkien variety keeps all the distinctions that Middle Chinese had. Zhangzhou keeps 陽韻 apart from 鍾 (*iang* vs *iong*); Quanzhou keeps 魚 apart from 虞 and 侯 apart from 模; Penang and Zhangzhou keep 日 apart from 來; Lukang and Quanzhou keep the "voiced upper" tone. The rule here is simple: **if any reference variety keeps a distinction, keep it; never invent one that none of them has.** The result is a *mosaic*, not one dialect. It reads more distinctly than any single variety, and every distinction it makes is one that real speakers make somewhere. In practice that means about 1,759 distinct syllables where an Amoy-only reading gives about 1,396.

It is meant for reading and teaching classical Chinese aloud, for checking what a literary reading "should" be when dictionaries disagree or are silent, and as a base for study tools. It is not a reconstruction of any historical layer, and not a claim about how any one place reads.

### How well it works

Checked against 7,683 characters that have a single 廣韻 reading and data in dictionaries and survey tables. A match means the initial and the final each occur in some Hokkien reading of that character (tone ignored).

| | all | top 1000 | top 3000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| rules only | 86.5% | 98.0% | 95.0% |
| with the exception list | 88.5% | 100% | 98.1% |
| held out (rules retrained on half the characters, scored on the other half) | 86.1% | 96.1% | 89.0% |

"Top N" is by classical-Chinese frequency. The exception list was chosen from the same data, so its figures are in-sample; the hold-out row is the honest one for new characters. Rare characters are where it is weakest, and those are mostly rime-book-only readings that few speakers would know either way.

## Using it

The file is a deriver script for [NK2028 tshet-uinh-deriver](https://github.com/nk2028/tshet-uinh-js) (tshet-uinh 0.15.x). Paste it into a deriver (for example the NK2028 web tool) or load it with the `tshet-uinh` npm package.

- Output: POJ with the usual extensions (o͘, ir, irn, eng/ek). Tone is the Middle Chinese class: 清/濁 × 平上去入, with 全濁上 as tone 6 written with a caron (ǎ ě ǐ ǒ ǔ).
- Option **例外** (on by default): about 216 characters whose rule output is not a syllable any variety has get the nearest real syllable (頭 thiô, 米 bí, 歲 sòe, 大 tāi, 不 put). It needs the character itself (字頭); without it the rules decide. Turn it off for pure rule output.

## Details (for readers who know the 廣韻)

**Initials** (聲母表) are a hand-written table, first match wins. 非敷奉 → h, 微 stays b; 日 → j kept apart from 來 l.

**Finals** (韻母表), first match wins, in this order:
1. 鑲嵌 — the mosaic finals: 陽韻開口 *iang* (Zhangzhou), 侯韻 *io* (Quanzhou), 痕/殷韻 *irn* (Quanzhou ɯn). Unconditional.
2. 手動 — hand rules that keep a contrast (魚 *ir*, 灰韻 *oe* outside 端/精/來, 齊韻幫組 *e*, 侵韻莊組 *om*, and others).
3. 補充 — a few hand overrides of the generated table.
4. 自動 — the modal literary final of the Amoy 甘字典 per 韻/呼/等/類, generated by script. Generated, not hand-edited.
5. 後備 — per-韻 fallback.

Entering-tone syllables share 韻/等/呼 with their level-tone partners, so one table serves all four tones and the coda is converted afterwards.

**What the mosaic adds** (distinct syllables, out of 3,804 小韻): tone 6 +106; 陽 *iang* +61; 日 j +53; 魚 *ir* +47; 侯 *io* +43; 痕殷 *irn* +23; 灰 *oe* +10; 齊幫組 *e* +8; 侵莊組 *om* +8.

**Exceptions** replace the initial and final of one character, never the tone. Characters outside the 3,000 most frequent get an exception only if the reading is attested outside the 十五音 rime-book lineage (甘字典, 雅俗通, Medhurst, and Wiktionary, which often copies them). Every row was reviewed by hand; colloquial words written with rare characters, Mandarin-shaped readings and variants of characters the rules already handle were dropped.

**Still merged** because no reference variety separates them in literary readings, or POJ cannot write the difference: 支/脂/之, 歌/豪, 清/青/蒸/庚三, 登/庚二/耕 (*eng/ek*; the 彙音妙悟 keeps them apart as *irng* but almost nobody reads it so), 仙/先, 覃/談, 知=端, 精=章=莊, 邪=心, 侯幫滂並=模.

**Known limits:** tone is always the Middle Chinese class, so readings that moved tone series keep the old tone (係 hè for usual hē, 站 chàm for chām). Characters whose dictionary reading belongs to a variant or another word at a different 音韻地位 are left to the rules on purpose.

Full sources, citations, design notes and the evaluation method are in the comment header of `hokkien-wendu-poj.js` (English with a Traditional Chinese translation).

## 簡介（中文）

本腳本為任何漢字推導一個「理想化」的閩南語文讀，用白話字書寫，方便用閩南語朗讀文言文。方法：從《廣韻》音韻地位出發，套用規則。設計原則是「鑲嵌」：只要廈門、臺灣、漳州、泉州、檳城（及鹿港第六聲）任何一個保留某個中古對立，就保留；沒有任何方言有的對立，絕不憑空創造。因此它比任何單一方言分得更細（約 1,759 個相異音節，廈門單點約 1,396），而每個區別在某處都有人這樣讀。它不是任何單一歷史層次的重建。使用方式、規則與評測詳見上文與腳本開頭的註解。
